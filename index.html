About Me

I'm an electrical engineer passionate about solving complex engineering challenges in aerospace and energy systems. My professional experience includes designing power electronics, embedded hardware, and PCB-based electronic systems supporting space and mission-critical aerospace and defense applications where reliability, performance, and precision are essential. I earned a B.S. in Electrical Engineering with a minor in Energy Engineering from the University of Colorado Boulder. Prior to beginning my engineering career, I served seven years in the United States Army, including a deployment in support of Operations Inherent Resolve and Spartan Shield. My technical interests include power electronics, PCB design and manufacturing, embedded systems, RF engineering, and high-reliability system design. I currently hold an active U.S. security clearance.
